Update history

What each version added and fixed. The App Store’s What’s New shows the latest version; the earlier ones are kept here.

2.1.0iPhone and iPad · in review

Submitted November 25, 2026
  • Hanbyeol Viewer comes to iPhone and iPad. It is the same app as on the Mac, so there is nothing more to buy.
  • Library, reading, Read Aloud, translation and the Wordbook, highlights and notes, lecture recording, the Assistant, document tools, comics and music all come along.
  • Only on iPhone and iPad: Scan Document, Continuous Scroll, Apple Pencil highlighting and Highlighter Mode, tap a mark to delete it, the recording on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, and Light View for large PDFs.
  • Mac: Read Aloud keeps reading when the sound output changes, for example when you connect AirPods.

2.0.1

November 22, 2026
  • PDFs up to 1 GB now open (512 MB in 2.0.0).
  • PDFs with many pages and large scanned PDFs no longer fail to open.
  • Extract Pages · Merge PDFs · Batch Convert are faster and use less memory with large PDFs.
  • Values you fill in on a PDF form are kept in copies you save or share.

2.0.0Major update

November 21, 2026
  • New design: move between Library, Lecture Notes and Wordbook in the sidebar; open the Assistant, Transcription and Notes beside the document.
  • Library shelves: recent documents, Favorites and your collections on shelves, showing how far you have read.
  • Lecture recording: record a class and it is transcribed on your Mac, with the page you were on. Click a sentence to hear it again.
  • Assistant: Apple Intelligence summarizes documents, answers questions with the pages it used, and makes Plain Words, Key Terms, Review Quiz and Rewrite.
  • Pictures: give Library covers and Wordbook words a picture made with Image Playground.
  • Larger PDFs (up to 512 MB). Bars beside the song title move with the music.
  • Requires macOS 26 or later from this version.

1.3.0

November 19, 2026
  • Read-aloud highlight: the sentence being read is highlighted and followed.
  • Sense Groups split a long English sentence into groups of meaning.
  • Word Study Sheet: turn your words into an A4 sheet to save as PDF or print.
  • Go to Source Document, and Undo Delete in the Wordbook.
  • Fixed printing, and sentences over two lines being translated line by line.

1.2.0

November 17, 2026
  • Open password-protected PDFs, and save a PDF with a password.
  • App Lock asks for Touch ID or your Mac password when the app opens.
  • All Notes gathers every document's highlights, underlines and notes, and exports them.

1.1.0

November 15, 2026
  • Quick Look in Finder: preview HWP and HWPX files with the Space bar; file icons show the first page.
  • Wordbook review cards with pronunciation, Export for Anki, and editing saved words.
  • Listen in the translation popover repeats the original and translation 1 to 3 times.

1.0.0First release

November 14, 2026
  • First released as a Mac viewer for HWP and HWPX, PDF, Word documents and comics (CBZ, CBR).
  • Read Aloud with 10 voices and a Sleep Timer, translation of selected text, the Wordbook and Translation Documents.
  • Save HWP and HWPX as PDF, PDF as HWPX or Word; Extract Pages, Merge PDFs and Batch Convert.
  • Highlights, underlines and notes; contents, bookmarks and search; music while you read.
